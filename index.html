CHANGELOG

v0.0.10 - New Look

Hand shaped pixel art for every character, every weapon and armor piece drawn on the hero, full animation sets with squash and stretch, animated spell effects. Story scenes with dialogues, portraits and illustrated boards. Tile terrain with platforms, walls and chasms, camera moving both ways. Parallax backgrounds, 2D lighting, bloom, reflections on ice, footprints and blood on snow. New interface with portraits, settings with graphics quality, difficulty, key binding, gamepad and accessibility options. Installable and playable offline.

v0.0.9 - Arsenal

Axe, spear, daggers, frost staff and short bow. Ice, fire and piercing arrows. Gear rarity with random bonuses, legendary Alpha Fang and Chief's Crown. Gloves, boots, ring and amulet slots. Item comparison. New foes: mimic, looter, snow lynx, poacher.

v0.0.8 - Talents

Talent tree with Warrior, Hunter and Frost branches and active skills. Random enemy spawns, area levels, bleeding, burning and other status effects.

v0.0.7 - Saves

Progress saved in the browser with continue, run summary, first-time tips, photo mode and credits.

v0.0.6 - Lore

Story scenes, experience and leveling up, hit sounds, particles and screen shake.

v0.0.5 - World

Winter Forest, Ice Cave and Castle Ruins. Slippery ice, falling icicles, chests and quests.

v0.0.4 - Battle

Archers, shamans, the Alpha Wolf boss and mana skills: frost strike and ice shield.

v0.0.3 - Loot

Enemy loot, inventory with gear, bow and arrows, merchant.

v0.0.2 - Mobile

Touch controls for phones.

v0.0.1

First playable version.
